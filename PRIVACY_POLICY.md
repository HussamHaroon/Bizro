# Bizro Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 5 September 2026**

Bizro ("we", "our") is a voice-first digital khata (ledger) service for small
retailers in Pakistan, delivered over WhatsApp. This policy explains what data
we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. A short Urdu summary
follows the English text.

## What we collect

- **Your WhatsApp phone number and display name** — used to identify your
  Bizro account when you message the Bizro number.
- **Voice notes you send us** — so we can transcribe them into ledger
  entries. Recordings are processed by our speech-to-text provider solely to
  produce text, and are stored only as needed to keep an audit trail of what
  was recorded.
- **Photos you send us** (e.g. receipts, bills) — processed by OCR to
  pre-fill expense entries, with a price-sanity check before anything is
  saved.
- **Transaction records you create** — credit given (udhaar), sales,
  expenses, and settlements, with counterparties' names as you speak them.
- **Credit Readiness Reports** generated from your own history. These are
  shared only with you unless you explicitly choose to share them.

## How we use your data

- To keep your ledger and produce confirmations and reports **for you**.
- Message content is processed by AI models (speech recognition, text
  understanding) operated by our model providers strictly to generate your
  ledger entries. Providers may not use your content to train their models.
- We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** share it with lenders or
  any third party without your explicit instruction.

## Retention & deletion

Your ledger stays in your Bizro account until you ask us to delete it.
Send **"data delete"** in your Bizro chat or email us and we will remove your
account and records within 30 days.

## Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WhatsApp). Access to the production
database is restricted to the Bizro service account. Inbound media is
size-capped and type-checked. Every ledger line stores an audit trail
(source model, raw transcript, timestamps).
